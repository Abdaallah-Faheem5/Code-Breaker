          PLAYER HANDBOOK

          Your vault protocol.

          Answer 24 questions across Rookie, Hacker, and Code Breaker. Question 24 is the final vault challenge. You have 20 minutes.

          • Pick, then lock. Select one of four cards and press Lock answer. Every question allows one submission. A fresh shuffled selection is made for each game, with no repeats across levels.
          • Learn and continue. Correct answers earn the displayed points. Wrong answers earn zero; both show an explanation and let you continue.
          • Three hints total. Each costs 75 points from your running total, including future earnings. Your displayed score never goes below zero. One hint per question.
          • Collect three seals. Their digits appear in your notebook after their questions, regardless of your answer. Use them to solve the final vault code.
          • Open the vault. Submit the correct final answer before time expires to win. A wrong final answer or timeout ends the run.
          • Compete fairly. Highest score wins; equal scores use the shorter completion time. Only victories qualify for your personal best. There is no time bonus.

          Keyboard: A–D or 1–4 selects, arrow keys move between answers, Enter submits. After feedback, Enter continues. Tab and Space also work. The timer continues in dialogs and hidden tabs.

          RESET PROTOCOL

          Start a fresh mission?

          Your current score and progress will reset. Your personal best stays saved.
